Reading position is now kept per surah, juz, hizb and page, and reopening one
from the library returns to where you stopped.

Book mode: long surahs open straight away instead of freezing, the verse
reference and progress bar follow the page, ayahs can be bookmarked, and tapping
one shows its translation.

A key for the tajweed colours in Settings.

Choosing a reciter while a recitation is playing now changes the voice without
losing your place.

Downloads can be cancelled, a failed one says so and offers to retry, and
downloading a whole reciter asks first and tells you how large it is.

Playback failures are explained instead of leaving a spinner.

Bookmarks show which surah each verse is from.

The playback range picker shows verse numbers rather than list positions.

Warmer selection colours on the sepia page, larger touch targets, screen reader
labels, and a layout that fits landscape and tablets.
