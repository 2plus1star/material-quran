Material Quran is a Quran reader.

The full Arabic text is stored in the app. You do not need a connection to
read.

WHAT IT DOES

Uthmani Arabic text.
English translation after each ayah, with the publisher footnotes.
Tajweed colors, so you can see the recitation rules in the text.
Recitation audio from 16 reciters.
The ayah being recited is highlighted as it plays.
Bookmarks.
Book mode, where the ayahs flow continuously like a printed page.

NAVIGATION

Browse by surah, juz, hizb, or page.
The app remembers where you stopped reading.

AUDIO

Play one ayah, a range of ayahs, or a whole surah.
Repeat each ayah 1, 3, or 5 times, or without stopping.
Download a surah to listen without a connection.
Playback continues when the screen is off. Controls are on the lock screen.

READING

Adjust the Arabic text size.
Sepia, light, and dark reading themes, and an AMOLED black option.
Translation can be turned off.

SOURCES

Quran text: Tanzil Project, tanzil.net, used unchanged.
Translation: Noor International (Saheeh), from QuranEnc.com, used unchanged.
Tajweed data: cpfair/quran-tajweed, CC BY 4.0.
Fonts: Noto Sans Arabic and Baloo Bhaijaan 2, SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Recitation audio is not included in the app. It is downloaded from
everyayah.com when you choose a reciter. Each recording belongs to its
reciter and producer.
