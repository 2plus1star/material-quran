Privacy Policy — Material Quran

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Material Quran is published by threestar. You can reach us about anything in this policy at twoplusonestar@gmail.com.

This is the privacy policy for the Material Quran Android application ("the app").

The short version

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We run no servers and receive nothing about you. We have no way to know you installed it.

The app does use the network for one thing: downloading recitation audio, and only when you ask for it. That request goes to a third party, not to us. Details below.

What the app stores on your device

Data Purpose
Your settings (translation on or off, book mode, tajweed colours, text size, theme, chosen reciter) Remembering your preferences
Your bookmarks So you can return to marked verses
Your last reading position So the app reopens where you stopped
Downloaded recitation audio Listening without a connection

All of it stays on your device. Uninstalling the app, or using "Clear storage" in Settings > Apps > Material Quran, removes everything permanently. Nothing is held anywhere else, so there is nothing else to request or erase.

The Quran text, the translation and the tajweed data ship inside the app. Reading requires no connection and no network access at all.

Android backup. If you have Android backup enabled, your settings, bookmarks and reading position may be copied to your own Google Drive backup, under your Google account and Google's privacy policy. Downloaded audio is deliberately excluded from backup, because it can run to gigabytes.

Recitation audio, please read this

Recitation audio is not included in the app. When you play or download a reciter, the app requests the audio files from everyayah.com, a third-party site we do not operate.

Like any web request, that request tells everyayah.com your IP address and which files you asked for. We never see it and we receive no copy of it. everyayah.com publishes no privacy policy or terms of use that we could find, so we cannot tell you what, if anything, they log.

If you would rather make no network requests at all, do not download or stream a reciter. Everything else in the app works fully offline.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.
  • It contains no advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or attribution SDK.
  • It has no user accounts and never asks you to sign in.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • It does not use your data for profiling or automated decision-making.

If Google Play sends us a crash report through Android vitals, it arrives from Google in aggregate, anonymised form. We receive no identifier that could connect it to you.

Permissions the app requests, and why

Permission Why
INTERNET To download or stream recitation audio, only when you ask
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK To keep a recitation playing when you leave the app or turn off the screen
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show the playback notification with its controls
WAKE_LOCK To stop the device sleeping mid-recitation during playback

The app requests no location, storage, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

Children

Material Quran is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Because no data leaves your device at our direction, there is no server to breach and no database of users to expose. On-device data is protected by Android's application sandbox and your device's own encryption.
